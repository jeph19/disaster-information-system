DRRM Noveleta Safety Guidelines

Official sector recommendations tailored to today's active heat risk level.

Heat Exhaustion

Requires Immediate Intervention
  • Heavy sweating, paleness, or fatigue
  • Fast but weak pulse rate
  • Dizziness, headache, nausea, or vomiting
  • Muscle cramps and heat exhaustion stiffness
First Aid: Move to a shaded/air-conditioned room, sip water slowly, and apply cool wet towels to the neck or armpits.

Heat Stroke (MEDICAL EMERGENCY)

Life-Threatening - Call Rescue Immediately
  • High body temperature (> 40°C or 104°F)
  • Hot, red, dry skin (absence of sweating)
  • Rapid, strong pulse & severe mental confusion
  • Loss of consciousness or fainting
Critical Action: Call MDRRMO Rescue Hotline immediately! Cool the victim with water or ice packs while waiting for the ambulance.
